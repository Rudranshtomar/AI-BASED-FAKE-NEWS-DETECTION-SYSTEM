Methodology

How It Works

A transparent, three-stage pipeline transforms raw news articles into calibrated, explainable verifications.

01

Enter News Content

Submit raw article text, a headline, or provide a supported news URL. The system automatically cleans, strips markup, and isolates the core reporting text.

02

AI Model Analyzes the Text

TF-IDF vectorization quantifies n-gram token distributions. A trained Logistic Regression classifier analyzes lexical variance against thousands of verified reports.

03

Review the Classification Result

Instantly receive the predicted classification (FAKE or REAL), calibrated probability percentage, and word-level explainability tokens that drove the model's decision.
